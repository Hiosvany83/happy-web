About VidaPlena

A family clinic built on listening, since 2012.

How it started

From two rooms to the neighbourhood's clinic

VidaPlena opened in 2012 with two consultation rooms, one nurse and a founding rule written on the wall: no patient leaves with an unanswered question. The idea was simple — listen first, publish every price, and follow up until the problem is actually solved.

Today we run six departments and our own laboratory, and we see around twenty thousand patients a year. What hasn't changed is the rule on the wall: it still hangs in the reception area, and new staff still read it on their first day.

Inside the clinic

Calm rooms, modern equipment

Our facilities were designed with patients, not just doctors: wide corridors, natural light, a children's corner in the waiting room and consultation rooms where conversations stay private.

The laboratory and treatment areas were renovated in 2024, so most diagnostics happen under one roof — no sending you across town with a paper slip.
